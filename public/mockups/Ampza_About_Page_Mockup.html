// About

Charging, for people. Not just for cars.

It's not vehicles that are going electric. It's people. Drivers, commuters, families, small business owners. Ampza exists to make that shift a little easier for the person behind the wheel, not just the machine in front of them.

// Mission & Vision

Mission

Predictable charging, every time. You know where the nearest station is, what it costs, and how long it takes, before you leave home. Built for the person driving, not just the vehicle.

Vision

Reliability first, scale second. A small, carefully tested network today, earning trust one station at a time, not chasing pins on a map.

// Our Symbol

The moment of connection

Two panels, and a bolt striking between them. The grey is everything ordinary about a journey: the parking spot, the wait, the routine. The yellow is what changes the moment you plug in.

The bolt doesn't sit quietly on one side. It cuts across both, because that's what a good charging network should feel like, not a separate errand, just a beat inside a normal day.

// Reliability by design

Making "available" mean "working".

Installed is not the same as operational. These are the standards we propose to hold ourselves to. They are targets, not a claim of current performance or a mandated standard.

≥97%
monthly operational uptime per connector (proposed)
≤5 min
to detect a detectable fault
≤15 min
to acknowledge a critical fault
100%
monthly reporting coverage

We will monitor live status, OCPP telemetry where supported, alerts and escalation, and a monthly dashboard of uptime, downtime, successful-session rate, recurring faults and mean time to repair.

// Where we are

Early days, on purpose

We're not pretending to be a mature network yet. Right now, that means a small founding team, a handful of partner locations being finalized, and a lot of direct conversations with our first users and hosts.

That's deliberate. We'd rather get the basics right with a small network than rush a large one that doesn't work.

We're starting in Tamil Nadu and Kerala, with broader South India after the model is proven.

PHASE 1
MONTHS 0–6

Build the platform

App built and tested, first host sites signed, hardware supplier chosen and tariff and connection cost confirmed per site. Evidence: signed site agreements, supplier quote, working app demo.

PHASE 2
MONTHS 6–12

First stations live

A small, tested set of locations, with uptime tracked on every station. Evidence: monthly uptime and sessions report for each station.

PHASE 3
MONTHS 12–24

Expand city by city

Usage compared with the 8-car target, payback recalculated from real costs, partner terms tested. Evidence: station-level economics shared with partners and investors.

No new city until existing stations meet the targets we set. Timings are proposed and depend on funding and site approvals.
