What has to be true. Local traffic supports about 8 sessions a day at the site. Uptime stays high enough that drivers return. Host terms and connection charges are fixed before launch.

What these figures do not include. Gross contribution is customer billing minus energy cost, before fixed and demand charges, host share, maintenance, staffing, software, payment fees, financing, taxes, downtime and ramp-up. GST is not considered. Simple payback is investment divided by monthly gross contribution: an illustrative planning metric, not a forecast or guaranteed return.

Tariff context. ₹20/kWh sits in the middle of KSEB's published ₹16.50 to ₹23.23 per kWh range for DC fast charging (before GST), and a 2026 market review puts most public tariffs at about ₹18 to ₹22. The ₹8.50/kWh electricity rate is a rounded average of illustrative Tamil Nadu and Kerala estimates, not an official tariff or site bill.
