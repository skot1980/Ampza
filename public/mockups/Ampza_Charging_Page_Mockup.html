07. Questions

Frequently Asked

How is pricing calculated?

You pay per kWh consumed, shown upfront on the app before you start charging. No separate connection or idle fees at launch.

Do I need a separate RFID card?

No RFID card is needed at launch. AmpTap lets you start a session by tapping your phone. An optional Ampza RFID card is planned after launch: tap a card to start a session with no app needed at the charger, even when your phone has little signal.

What if a station is unavailable when I arrive?

Live status is shown in the app, so you'll know before you leave. We're working on live occupancy updates as the network grows.

How do I get support during a charging session?

In-app chat connects you directly to our support team. During our early launch phase, that's a small, responsive team, not a call queue.
