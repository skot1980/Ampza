// General

General

What is Ampza?

Ampza is an EV charging network being built for Tamil Nadu and Kerala, around one promise: if a station is shown as available, it should work. You will find stations, see the price, start a session and track the charge in one app. We are pre-launch, and building the app and our first station partnerships now.

Where will Ampza stations be?

We are starting in Tamil Nadu and Kerala, with stations at host sites such as malls, hotels, offices and restaurants. Stations appear on the app map as they go live. Join the waitlist to hear when one opens near you.

When does Ampza launch?

We have not announced a launch date. We would rather open a small number of tested stations than rush a large network. Join the waitlist and we will tell you first.

Which vehicles can charge at Ampza stations?

Our launch stations support these connectors:

  • CCS2 (DC fast): the main standard for most new electric cars and SUVs sold in India.
  • Type 2 (AC): slower charging for longer stops.
  • CHAdeMO (DC fast): at select stations, for compatible vehicles.

Check your vehicle's charging port before you go; the app shows the connector types at each station.

How long does charging take?

It depends on your battery size, how full it is, and the maximum rate your car accepts. As a rough guide, DC fast charging typically takes around 30 to 60 minutes to go from 20% to 80%, while AC charging takes several hours. Your vehicle manual has the figures for your model.

// App & account

App & account

How do I download the Ampza app?

The app is not live yet. When it launches, download links for the App Store and Google Play will be on this site. Join the waitlist to be notified.

How do I create an account?

Enter your mobile number, verify it with an OTP, and add your name and vehicle details. You do not need an RFID card to start.

Can I use Ampza without the app?

At launch the app is the main way to charge. An optional Ampza RFID card, which starts a session with a single tap and no app at the charger, is planned after launch.

How do I update or delete my personal data?

Email hello@ampza.in from your registered address, or use the in-app request option when available. See our Privacy Policy for your rights and how long we keep data.

// Starting and tracking a charge

Starting and tracking a charge

How do I start a charge?
  1. Open the app and choose a station and connector.
  2. Plug the connector into your car.
  3. Tap your phone at the station with AmpTap to start the session.
  4. Track speed, cost and time remaining in the app.

See How to charge for the full walkthrough.

What is AmpTap?

AmpTap lets you start a session by tapping your phone at the station. No separate RFID card is required at launch.

What is Auto Charge, and will it work with my car?

With Auto Charge, charging can start on its own when you plug in, with no tap needed. It depends primarily on your vehicle model, so check that your car is supported and activate the feature if required. Station or network capability may also affect availability. Auto Charge is planned for launch.

What is the Ampza RFID card?

An optional card linked to your account. Tap it to start a session with no app needed at the charger. It still works when your phone has little signal, and cards can be assigned to family members or fleet drivers. It is planned after launch.

How do I know a charger is available before I go?

The app shows live station status, connector type, distance and price on the map. We are working on live occupancy updates as well. Reliability is what we measure, and we are aiming for at least 97% monthly uptime per connector.

Can I stop a session early?

Yes. Stop it from the app or at the charger any time. You pay only for the energy delivered.

// Payments, wallet & invoices

Payments, wallet & invoices

How is the price calculated?

You pay per kWh of energy delivered. The tariff is shown in the app before you start. See Pricing for how it works.

Which payment methods can I use?

UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking and the Ampza wallet. The options available are shown in the app at checkout.

How do I top up my wallet?
  1. Open Wallet in the app.
  2. Enter the top-up amount.
  3. Pay with UPI, card or net banking.

The wallet can be used only for Ampza charging, and balances cannot be transferred to another user.

Will I get an invoice?

Yes, for every session, in the app and by email. You can add your business details to your profile so that invoices carry them.

How do I get a refund?

See our Cancellation & Refund Policy. In short, we refund sessions that did not start, stopped because of a fault on our side, or were billed incorrectly, back to your original payment method.

// If something goes wrong

If something goes wrong

What should I do if my charge does not start?
  1. Check the connector is fully plugged in and your vehicle is ready to charge.
  2. Try starting the session again in the app.
  3. If it still fails, contact support from the app, or call us on +91-9842910644.

If you were charged but the session did not start, we will refund it.

I was charged but my session stopped early. What now?

Raise a refund request with the station, date and session ID, as soon as you can after the session. We check the station and payment records and refund any amount for energy not delivered.

A station showed as available but did not work. Why?

That is exactly what we are building Ampza to avoid. Please report it to us so we can fix it; we track uptime, session success and fault restoration for every station.

How do I contact support?

Email support@ampza.in or call +91-9842910644. In-app chat will be available at launch. See Contact us.

How do I make a complaint?

Use the grievance form on our Contact page. Tell us what happened and we will look into it and get back to you.

// Hosting & partnerships

Hosting & partnerships

How do I host an Ampza station at my property?

Tell us about your site through the partner enquiry form. We look for strong repeat footfall and dwell time, a suitable power connection or capacity to add one, parking for charger bays with safe access, and a visible, easy-to-reach location. You provide space and power; Ampza provides hardware, app listing and support, and you share in session revenue.

What does it cost to become a host or franchise partner?

Commercial terms are agreed with each partner, in writing, before a station goes live. Start with the partner enquiry form and the founding team will get in touch.

Can I list my existing charger on Ampza?

Yes, if it is OCPP-compatible. We check compatibility for each charger model, then list it so drivers can find it and pay through the app.

Do you offer charging plans for fleets?

Yes. Fleet and corporate plans include consolidated billing, priority access and usage visibility. See Business or contact us.
