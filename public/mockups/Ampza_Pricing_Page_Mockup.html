// Pricing

Pay per kWh. See the price before you start.

No subscriptions and no hidden fees. You pay for the energy delivered to your vehicle, and the tariff for each station is shown in the app before you plug in.

// How pricing works

Four things to know

01

Per kWh, not per minute

You are billed for the energy delivered to your vehicle, so a slower-charging car is not penalised for time.

02

Price shown upfront

Connector type, distance and price per kWh are visible on the map before you drive over, and again before you start.

03

Tariffs can vary

Prices can differ by station, connector and time of day. A change applies only to sessions that start after it.

04

Taxes and invoices

GST and other taxes apply as shown at billing, and you get an invoice for every session.

// In the app

What you see, and when

No surprises: the price is on screen before you plug in, and the cost is tracked while you charge.

Before you start

The tariff

The price per kWh for that station and connector, shown on the map and again before you confirm.

While you charge

Live cost

Energy delivered, charging speed and the cost so far, updating as the session runs.

After you charge

Your invoice

A receipt for every session showing the energy delivered, the amount and any applicable taxes.

// Payments

How you pay

Payment methods

UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking, and your Ampza wallet. The options available are shown in the app at checkout. Card and UPI details are handled by the payment gateway, not stored by Ampza.

Ampza wallet

Top up in the app and pay for sessions with one tap. Wallet money can be used only for Ampza charging. Unused balance can be refunded under our refund policy.

Charged for a session that did not work? We refund failed sessions. See our Cancellation & Refund Policy or the payments FAQs.
